FAQ

Before you order

Anything else, message me on Fiverr.

Do I need to ship you my product?

No. 2–4 clear photos and your product link are enough. I build every scene from those.

Will it look like my real product?

Yes. Your exact product stays the hero: same shape, colour, print and logo. I only change the setting and the person using it.

Which platforms are the ads for?

TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook, and product pages on Shopify, Amazon and Etsy. The main delivery is a 9:16 MP4; 1:1 and 16:9 are available as extras.

Is the music safe to use in ads?

Yes. Every ad uses licensed music, so you can run it as a paid ad.

Why three hooks in the Standard package?

The hook decides whether anyone watches the rest. Three different hooks on the same product let you run an A/B test and put your budget behind the winner.

Can I see an idea before I pay?

Yes. Message me with your product and I'll tell you the first hook I'd test for it.
